How to play

  • Bop the moles! Tap a mole when it pops out of a mound — +10 points, times your combo.
  • Build combos: bop moles in quick succession to raise your multiplier up to ×5. Let it lapse and the combo resets.
  • Golden mole: rare and shiny — worth a flat +50 points. Grab it fast!
  • Hands off the bunny! The baby bunny is a friend of the garden. Bopping it costs −15 points and breaks your combo.
  • 60 seconds on the clock. Moles get quicker and sneakier as time runs down. Missing one costs nothing — just keep bopping!
